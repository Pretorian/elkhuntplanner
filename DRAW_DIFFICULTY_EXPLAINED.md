# Draw Difficulty Explained

## What is a "Draw"?

In western states, popular hunting units use a **lottery system** to allocate limited licenses. You apply for a hunting license, and if selected in the random draw, you get a tag for that unit. This system manages hunting pressure and protects wildlife populations.

## Draw Difficulty Levels

Your Elk Hunt Planner categorizes units by how hard they are to draw:

### 🔴 **Hard Draw** (High Difficulty)
**What it means:**
- Very low odds of drawing a tag (often <5%)
- Takes many years of applying to draw (sometimes 5-20+ years)
- Premium units with exceptional elk hunting
- Limited tags issued (sometimes only 10-50 tags)
- May require maximum preference points

**Why it's hard:**
- Trophy-quality bulls
- High success rates (elk density)
- Easy access or prime terrain
- Historical reputation
- Everyone wants to hunt there

**Strategy:**
- Build preference points over years
- Apply as a group (sometimes better odds)
- Consider these for "dream hunts" later in life
- Use as 1st choice only when you have max points

**Example:**
A unit with 10 tags and 500 applicants = 2% odds

---

### 🟡 **Moderate Draw** (Medium Difficulty)
**What it means:**
- Moderate odds (typically 10-30%)
- May draw within 2-5 years
- Good elk hunting but not elite
- Reasonable number of tags available
- Requires some preference points

**Why it's moderate:**
- Good elk populations but not exceptional
- Decent access but may require hiking
- Mix of public and private land
- Quality hunting with reasonable odds

**Strategy:**
- Good choice for mid-points (3-7 points)
- Balance of opportunity and quality
- Can be successful with research
- 2nd or 3rd choice option

**Example:**
GMU 12 (Flat Tops) - Your 4th choice is actually a moderate draw, making it your most realistic option on your 2026 application

---

### 🟢 **Low-Moderate Draw** (Lower Difficulty)
**What it means:**
- Better odds (30-60%+)
- Often draw within 1-3 years, sometimes first try
- Good hunting opportunities
- More tags available
- Lower preference points needed

**Why it's easier:**
- Good elk numbers but requires more work
- More challenging terrain or access
- Less well-known units
- May have more private land complications
- Still quality hunting if you know where to go

**Strategy:**
- Great for first-time out-of-state hunters
- Good for building points while hunting
- 3rd or 4th choice selections
- "Hunt while you wait" for premium units

**Example:**
GMU 62 (Uncompahgre Plateau) - Your 3rd choice has decent odds as a non-resident

## Understanding Your Odds

### Preference Points System
Most states (including Colorado) use **preference points**:
- Each year you apply and DON'T draw = 1 point
- More points = higher priority in draw
- Point creep: As units get more popular, required points increase
- Weighted points: Some states square your points for better odds

### Colorado Draw Mechanics
**Weighted preference points:**
- If you have 5 points, your name goes in the hat **25 times** (5²)
- Someone with 10 points gets **100 entries** (10²)
- This dramatically improves odds as you build points

**Draw order:**
1. First, draw for 1st choice applications
2. Then 2nd choice (if tags remain)
3. Then 3rd choice
4. Then 4th choice

### Reading Draw Odds

**From GoHunt or CPW:**
```
Unit 12 - 2nd Choice Resident
Points needed: 4-7
Draw odds with 5 points: ~25%
```

This means:
- You need roughly 4-7 points to have a decent shot
- With 5 points, 1 in 4 applicants drew
- 75% of applicants with 5 points did NOT draw

## Factors That Affect Draw Difficulty

### 1. **Resident vs Non-Resident**
- Residents: Better odds, more tags allocated
- Non-Residents: Harder draws, tag caps (often 20% of tags)
- Example: A "moderate" resident draw might be "hard" for non-residents

### 2. **Season Type**
- **Archery (Sept)** - Easier to draw, longer season
- **Rifle (Oct-Nov)** - Harder to draw, shorter seasons, better success
- **Muzzleloader** - Varies by unit

### 3. **Tag Type**
- **Either Sex (bull or cow)** - Harder to draw
- **Antlerless only** - Much easier, sometimes leftover tags

### 4. **Special Restrictions**
- Antler restrictions (e.g., "4 points or 5" brow tine")
- Don't affect draw odds but affect hunting strategy

## Common Questions

### Q: If I don't draw my 1st choice, do I lose my points?
**A:** No! In Colorado, you only lose points if you actually draw a tag. If you don't draw any of your 4 choices, you gain another point.

### Q: Should I apply for hard-to-draw units?
**A:** Yes, as your 1st choice! You're building points anyway. Might as well take a shot. Put realistic units as 2nd-4th choices.

### Q: What are "leftover" tags?
**A:** Tags that didn't get drawn by anyone. Available first-come, first-served after the draw. Usually easier units or less desirable seasons.

### Q: Can I hunt while building points?
**A:** Yes! This is the "hunt while you wait" strategy:
- Put dream units as 1st choice (to build points)
- Put drawable units as 3rd/4th choice
- If you draw 3rd/4th, you hunt this year
- If you don't draw anything, you gain a point for next year

## Point Creep Warning

**Point creep** = The phenomenon where required points for units increase each year.

**Why it happens:**
- More people applying
- Fewer people drawing (holding points)
- Older hunters with max points applying
- Units gaining popularity online

**Impact:**
- A unit that took 5 points in 2020 might take 8 points in 2025
- You might never "catch up" to premium units
- Need to reassess strategy every few years

**Solution:**
- Don't wait forever for dream units
- Hunt good units before they become impossible
- Consider "sleeper" units (underrated but good hunting)

## Unit Quality vs Draw Difficulty

**Don't assume hard = better hunting!**

Some units are hard to draw because:
- ✅ Genuinely great hunting
- ❌ Close to population centers (convenient)
- ❌ Famous from TV shows/influencers
- ❌ Easy access (road hunters)
- ❌ Historical reputation (may have declined)

Some units are easier to draw but still offer:
- ✅ Great elk populations
- ✅ Less hunting pressure
- ✅ Better trophy quality if you hike deep
- ✅ Underrated/unknown gems

**Do your research beyond just draw odds!**
